Spam Messages (Examples)

“Congratulations! You’ve won a ₹50,000 gift voucher—click the link now to claim!”

“Your account will be suspended today unless you verify your details at this link.”

“Get instant weight-loss pills with 90% discount! Limited stock—buy now!”

“You have been selected for a free iPhone 16! Pay ₹99 shipping to receive it.”

“Earn ₹10,000 per day working from home—no skills needed, just register here!”

Non-Spam (Normal) Messages

“Hey, are you free tomorrow? We need to discuss the project.”

“Your order has been shipped and should arrive by Monday.”

“Please send me the notes from yesterday’s class.”

“The meeting has been rescheduled to 3 PM today.”

“Reminder: submit your assignment before the deadline.”
